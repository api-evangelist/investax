# InvestaX

> InvestaX is a Singapore-based, MAS-licensed tokenization platform for real-world assets (RWAs), operated by IC SG Pte. Ltd. under a Capital Markets Services license (CMS100635) and a Recognised Market Operator license from the Monetary Authority of Singapore. It provides end-to-end infrastructure for issuance, distribution, secondary trading, and custody of security tokens, a Tokenization SaaS product for institutional issuers, and RWA investment products. Backed by 500 Global.
